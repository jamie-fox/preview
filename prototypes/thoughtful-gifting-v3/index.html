Frequently asked questions

  • Are your personalised mugs microwave and dishwasher safe?

    Our custom mugs are indeed both microwave and dishwasher safe. The only exception is our heat-changing mugs, which are microwave safe, but need to be hand washed to help preserve their effect.

    Close
  • What is the size and capacity of your photo mugs?

    Our mugs are 8.2 x 9.5 cm, and hold up to 285ml – perfect for a nice, satisfying hot beverage in your personalised cup.

    Close
  • Will the picture mug be able to withstand heat?

    When you fill them with hot liquid, our mugs do start to heat up – as all mugs do. But our high-quality printing makes sure that the photo on the mug is resistant to heat damage, and the sturdy handle will remain cool so you can sip your beverages safely.

    Close
  • How do you print the photo on the mug?

    During our custom mug printing process, your photos are embedded into the ceramic through a process known as sublimation that uses heat and special top-quality inks. Think of it like a big oven – essentially, your photos are baked into your personalised mugs so they won't come off.

    Close
  • How many photos can I print on my personalised photo mug?

    You can add up to 20 pictures per personalised mug.

    Close
  • Can I wrap an image fully around the printed mug?

    Yes – you can choose in the Editor whether you want to keep your photo on the mug on one side, or go panorama and wrap it all the way around.

    Close
  • Are these photo mugs suitable for promotional purposes or corporate branding?

    Of course, our Editor and printing options make it easy to add logos, slogans, branding – you name it. A batch of custom mugs is a fun way to get your name out there, perfect for corporate gifts, giveaways, stocking the office kitchen with personalised coffee cups, tea mugs and more.

    Close
  • How to create a personalised mug?

    Here's how to create your own printed mug in minutes:

    1. Pick your mug style (themed, heat-changing, etc.)
    2. Choose a theme design for creative inspiration
    3. Upload your photos
    4. Add text for that extra personal touch
    5. Preview your creation and add to cart
    Close
  • What should I write for some extra personalisation on my mug?

    Anything from cheeky quotes to heartfelt messages make for a nice touch to your personalised cup. A few ideas: "But first, coffee" – a classic for a custom coffee mug; "World's Best Dad" – Father's Day ready; "[Name]'s Mug – Hands Off" – a hit for personalised name mugs.

    Close
  • How long does it take to finish a personalised jigsaw?

    If you've done photo jigsaws before, a good rule of thumb is that if you double the number of pieces, you increase the time needed by four. So if a 100-piece puzzle takes you 30 minutes, a 200-piece puzzle might take around two hours. It's suggested that a 500-piece puzzle will take the average person about five or six hours. Experienced puzzlers can complete a 1,000-piece personalised jigsaw in about 10 hours, but the average person might need up to 24 hours.

    Close
  • What type of photo will make a jigsaw puzzle more or less difficult?

    Images with well-defined colours and areas of interest tend to make jigsaw puzzles easier, as you can assemble each part separately before putting the whole picture together. Good contrast in your photo helps, while murky and blurry images make things harder.

    Close
  • Can photo jigsaws be framed like photos or drawings?

    Yes, you can definitely turn your puzzle into a stylish accessory for your home. Use a special puzzle adhesive on both sides to ensure it stays secure. It's a good idea to mount your photo jigsaw onto a foam board for added stability before sliding it into a frame. Hang with an extra hook if you're worried about the weight.

    Close
  • Can I create a photo puzzle on the app?

    Absolutely. Make sure you have the app on your device, then log in to your Photobox account to save your photos – you can select them straight from your camera roll. Once saved, you can create your photo puzzle right away, or choose to create it on the app or your desktop later. Easy peasy.
